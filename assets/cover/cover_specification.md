## 1. Concept Narrative & Architecture

The visual identity of *Build with AI Agents* balances technical rigor with modern publishing prestige:
* **Background Canvas:** Ultra-deep matte carbon charcoal (`#0C0D10` / `#121316`) featuring subtle blueprint grid coordinate lines (10% opacity) that evoke architectural CAD diagrams and schematic precision.
* **Typographic Impact:**
  * **Title:** Heavy Swiss Grotesk uppercase sans-serif in crisp off-white (`#F8F9FA`), tracked slightly tight (-0.035em) to command immediate authority on digital catalog thumbnails (Amazon KDP, Gumroad, Leanpub).
  * **Subtitle:** Medium-weight grotesque sans in pure off-white, providing immediate contextual clarity for beginners and aspiring developers.
  * **Metadata Eyebrow:** Electric lime uppercase mono text establishing thematic domain (`PRACTICAL APPLICATIONS`).
* **Focal Geometry:**
  * A central three-dimensional wireframe polyhedron rendered in razor-sharp **Electric Lime** (`#B7FF00`).
  * The geometry symbolizes autonomous multi-agent orchestration: intersecting nodes, structured decision matrices, and clean algorithmic order emerging from conversational intent.
* **Spine & Back Cover (Print Wrap):**
  * Spine width: Calculated at 0.72 in for standard 50lb white paper at ~320 pages.
  * Running spine text: Bold uppercase title with author credit and publisher colophon.
